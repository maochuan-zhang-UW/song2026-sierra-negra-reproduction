# Observational candidate analysis

The seven-station PyOcto experiment contains 536 candidate events, not the paper's relocated catalog. `extract_candidate_windows.py` deconvolves station responses to ground velocity (m/s) on real 64-s contexts using a 0.5, 1, 22, 24 Hz stabilization taper, no water level, and a 5% time taper. A four-corner zero-phase 2–20 Hz filter and 50-Hz resampling precede 5-s windows beginning one second before assigned P/Z and S/N,E arrivals. The noise RMS window is −11 to −6 s; −6 to −1 s is a sensitivity case. Sixty-three 64-versus-128-s context comparisons give minimum waveform NCC 0.9999999999999714 and maximum FI change 3.20e−9. This checks these examples, not every possible context.

The mean station FI is log2(mean FFT amplitude at 9–15 Hz / mean FFT amplitude at 2–5 Hz), using P/Z windows with RMS SNR ≥2. FI <−2 identifies LP candidates. The resulting population contains 96 LP, 438 VT and two unclassified candidates. Station bootstrap bounds condition on the observed stations and processing; they do not represent total classification uncertainty.

The C++ kernel maximizes absolute normalized correlation over ±0.2 s while retaining its sign. Available component scores are averaged within each common station, then station scores receive equal weight. The primary experiment requires four common stations, an independently declared quality screen. Positive edges >0.7 connect events; components of at least ten events define families. One ten-event LP family occurs between 14:21:48 and 16:54:32 UTC, with mean recurrence 1018.18 s. Mean/std recurrence periodicity is 1.19869 using population standard deviation and 1.13014 using sample standard deviation. This does not reproduce the reported 29-family population. Zero-lag correlation finds no qualifying families; allowing only one common station yields one 17-member family. See the full sensitivity table.

Three negative network pairs qualify for waveform review. Signed channel scores and polarity-preserving overlay plots are provided. A best negative lag in narrow-band waveforms can reflect a phase shift; negative correlation alone does not establish reversal of the source mechanism. These pairs remain review candidates and are not equated to the paper's 11 pairs.

`prepare_matching_velocity.py` processes every acquired 8G station-hour with real 30-s context before hourly trimming. `match_candidate_templates.py` searches the actual full day with 447 templates having SNR-qualified data from at least four stations. The positive network threshold is max(9 × median(abs(network NCC)), 0.4). The MAD convention follows the [EQcorrscan source documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eqcorrscan/EQcorrscan/master/eqcorrscan/core/match_filter/matched_filter.py); the source study's exact version is unverified. Highest-score-first suppression and exclusion within two seconds of all known candidates are independently specified safeguards. Detection execution records report self-recovery, counts and limitations after completion. Additional detections inherit approximate template locations and primary FI, while `extract_matched_windows.py` also measures FI directly on real detection waveforms. No earthquake identity, magnitude or false-positive rate is assumed from correlation alone.

The completed expanded graph gives two LP families, 23 members, and five negative-pair review candidates with the primary four-station screen; the one-station sensitivity gives 14 families and 452 members. Matching thresholds reduce additional counts from 1007 at 0.4 NCC to 112 at 0.7. Approximate magnitudes exist for 1154 candidates. Independent OLS and RANSAC regressions are explicitly distinguished; no original RANSAC agreement is claimed.

The independent GNSS bend fit gives GV09 east timing 17:11 in a 14:30–19:15 window, with 17:09–17:24 window sensitivity. Other components are much less stable. This does not independently establish a unique intrusion onset.

A full-day phase-randomized shape control uses 20 templates (ten LP, ten VT), retaining individual channel Fourier amplitude spectra but independently randomizing phases with seed 20261009. At the identical network thresholds there are zero raw control peaks. This checks waveform-shape specificity; the smaller library and altered network coherence prevent converting it into an earthquake false-positive rate.
